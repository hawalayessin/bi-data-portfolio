## Car Sales

Excel-based Power BI dashboard showing year-to-date sales, average vehicle price,
vehicles sold and breakdowns by body type, manufacturer and region.

## How to review

View the screenshots directly or open the PBIX in Power BI Desktop. A data refresh
may require the original local source paths. The upstream datasets and their
redistribution licenses have not yet been documented, so this repository does not
add separate raw datasets. Source preparation scripts and exported DAX measures
are not included in the available assets and must be recovered from the original
work. See [provenance notes](DATA_SOURCES.md).

These assets were already public in my portfolio repository. The reports are
academic demonstrations, not claims of production work or commercial results.
